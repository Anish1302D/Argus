Hypersonic Thermal Protection System: Flight Qualification Assessment

Abstract: We report on the flight demonstration of an ultra-high temperature ceramic matrix composite.

Arc-Jet Facility Testing: Plasma arc-jet tests delivered 2,150 K surface temperatures for 180 seconds under simulated Mach 7 aerothermal conditions.

Limitations: However, contrary to initial programmatic targets, the system has not yet undergone actual suborbital sounding rocket flight demonstration. Flight tests remain unperformed due to launch range delays.
