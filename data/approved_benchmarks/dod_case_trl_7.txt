Tactical UAV Electronic Warfare Payload: System Prototype Demonstrated in an Operational Environment
DoD Technology Readiness Assessment Case #712

1. Operational System Prototype Demonstration
A full-scale system prototype payload was installed onto an operational MQ-9 Reaper aircraft to evaluate electronic countermeasures during live operational exercises.

2. Demonstration in an Operational Environment
The prototype was operated across 8 live flight sorties in an actual operational environment comprising contested electronic warfare ranges. System prototype demonstration in an operational environment verified real-time signal interception, automated emitter classification, and jamming response times under 50 ms. Operational pilots and mission commanders confirmed interoperability with existing tactical datalinks.

3. Assessment
The system prototype demonstrated in an operational environment meets all target thresholds. Final weapon system qualification testing (DT&E/OT&E) and military production configuration are underway.
