High-Altitude Stratospheric Balloon Demonstration of Aerosol Spectrometer Prototype
NASA Technical Publication (NASA-TP-2023-501239)

1. Engineering Prototype Overview
An engineering model and prototype aerosol spectrometer was constructed to representative operational flight form and mass specifications (12.4 kg, 28 VDC avionics bus).

2. Demonstration in a Relevant Environment
The prototype system was integrated onto a high-altitude scientific balloon gondola and flown at a float altitude of 34 km for 18 hours. This flight demonstration validated operational functionality in a relevant stratospheric environment under extreme cold (-65C) and low ambient pressure (6 hPa). Real-time optical telemetry was continuously transmitted to ground stations.

3. Maturity Rating
The technology was demonstrated in a relevant environment. Critical engineering prototype functionality was proven under representative flight conditions. Demonstration in an actual operational space satellite mission remains pending.
