Autonomous Surface Navigation LiDAR: Actual System Proven in Operational Use
NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory Mission Telemetry Report (JPL-PUB-2024-003)

1. Mission Operational Deployment
The autonomous navigation LiDAR payload has been in continuous, sustained operational service aboard planetary rover surface missions for over 36 consecutive months. 

2. Operational Mission Performance
The actual system is proven in operational use through repeated surface traversals exceeding 42 kilometers across rugged terrain. Operational mission telemetry demonstrates 99.98% hardware availability, zero fatal anomalies, and full compliance with tactical autonomous driving mission constraints. More than 120 commercial and scientific units are actively deployed in commercial and government flight programs.

3. Final Readiness Determination
The actual system proven in operational use has achieved the highest maturity level through sustained mission success. All operational maintenance history, commercial telemetry, and reliability metrics confirm full operational readiness.
