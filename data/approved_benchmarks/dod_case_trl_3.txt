Experimental Proof of Concept for Micro-Fluidic Phase-Change Cooling
DoD Technology Readiness Assessment (TRA) Case File

1. Experimental Proof of Concept
Active research and development was initiated to validate critical cooling characteristics through physical laboratory experiments. A bench-scale test setup was fabricated comprising a single micro-channel test fixture and dielectric working fluid.

2. Laboratory Measurement and Experimental Results
Bench-scale experiments were conducted across 45 test cycles. Laboratory measurements demonstrated heat removal rates of 120 W/cm2 with fluid pressure drop maintained within 15 kPa. The experimental data validates the physical feasibility of the underlying heat transfer equations.

3. Maturity Assessment
The technology satisfies experimental proof of concept. Components were tested individually on bench setups. Integration of these components into a functioning laboratory breadboard has not yet commenced.
