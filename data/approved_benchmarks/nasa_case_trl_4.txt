Integrated Optical Receiver Breadboard Laboratory Validation
NASA Technical Memorandum (NASA-TM-2022-220412)

1. Laboratory Breadboard Integration
Individual optical components—including the avalanche photodiode array, transimpedance amplifier, and optical bandpass filters—were integrated into a functioning low-fidelity laboratory breadboard system.

2. Laboratory Validation Testing
The integrated system was subjected to comprehensive laboratory validation under controlled ambient laboratory conditions. Testing over 200 continuous hours established functional interoperability between the integrated subsystems. The measured bit error rate (BER) met the target threshold of 1e-9 at a data throughput of 10 Gbps.

3. Assessment and Next Steps
The technology is validated in a laboratory environment. Individual elements operate cooperatively on a low-fidelity breadboard. Testing in a relevant simulated thermal vacuum environment has not yet been performed.
