Next-Generation ADS-B Flight Transceiver: System Completed and Qualified Through Test
Federal Aviation Administration Technical Center Report (DOT/FAA/TC-24/18)

1. Final Production System Qualification
Development of the final commercial hardware and avionics software was completed in production form. The actual system was subjected to formal qualification testing under RTCA DO-160G and DO-178C Level A standards.

2. Developmental and Operational Testing
The actual system was completed and qualified through test and demonstration. Testing included full environmental qualification, electromagnetic interference (EMI/EMC) certification, and 500 hours of flight testing on certified commercial airliners. Operating manuals, factory acceptance tests, and technical standard orders (TSO-C166b) were approved by regulatory authorities.

3. Readiness Level
The actual system completed and qualified through testing is certified for commercial aircraft production and installation. Sustained revenue airline operational service will validate long-term in-service reliability.
