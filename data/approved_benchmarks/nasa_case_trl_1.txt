Theoretical Foundations of Quantum Entangled Sensing for Gravitational Anomaly Detection
NASA Systems Engineering Reference Baseline Case

1. Executive Summary & Basic Scientific Principles
This technical investigation reports on the basic principles observed in non-local quantum state preservation for micro-gravity anomaly sensing. Initial mathematical derivations indicate a theoretical sensitivity improvement scaling with the Heisenberg limit rather than standard shot-noise limits.

2. Theoretical Formulations
Basic scientific principles were observed during mathematical modeling of correlated photon pairs passing through simulated gravitational gradients. Calculations were established using tensor calculus and published peer-reviewed theoretical literature. 

3. Status of Technology
At this juncture, only basic scientific principles have been observed and reported. Practical applications have not yet been evaluated, and no experimental proof of concept or physical prototype exists. The research remains in pure theoretical inquiry.
