Carbon Nanotube High-Conductivity Thermal Interface: Technology Concept Formulation
Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) Technology Readiness Case Study

1. Technology Concept Formulation
Following initial observation of thermal conductivity phenomena in aligned multi-walled carbon nanotubes, practical applications have been identified for aerospace avionics heat dissipation. An analytical concept report has been formulated detailing the thermal transfer mechanics.

2. Analytical Modeling and Concept Formulation
Analytical formulation of the concept was performed using finite element heat conduction modeling. The calculations demonstrate potential heat sink thermal resistance reduction of 35% compared to conventional copper shims.

3. Current Maturity and Validation Limitations
The practical application has been conceptually identified and analytical studies documented. However, no physical experiments or bench-scale testing have been conducted to date. Physical fabrication and laboratory validation remain future work.
